The colony wakes because someone stayed at the pumps.

Beyond the control-room glass, alien reeds bend around flooded channels and the first habitat windows glow through the grey morning. The Floodplain Keeper watches the levels, clears the next supply cycle and keeps clean water moving toward home. Out here, a quiet shift is a small promise kept to everyone beyond the sluice gate.
