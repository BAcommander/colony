Instrumental only, 60 seconds. Soft flowing synth ambient, beatless and free-time, warm analog pads with gentle chorus and a medium hall reverb. Mood: a sheltered water-reclamation control room on a colony world under grey skies; intimate, patient, quietly hopeful, gentle currents outside and steady care inside.

Start with three or four independently moving harmonic voices already present: rounded analog midrange pads, a soft reed-like synth tone, a light translucent upper layer and a restrained warm bass. Let sustained chords overlap through slow voice-leading, suspended seconds and open intervals, arriving occasionally at a warmer consonance. Suggest water through smooth timbral change and overlapping swells rather than any effect. Now and then a two- or three-note phrase surfaces in one voice and is softly answered by another before both dissolve.

Keep the midrange readable, bass supportive and centred, upper layer soft rather than sparkling, level even with no sudden entrances. End harmonically open and still moving, with no cadence.

No vocals, drums, percussion, arpeggios, sound effects, risers or climaxes.
