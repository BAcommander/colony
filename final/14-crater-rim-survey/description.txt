The caldera was ancient before the first homes appeared on its rim.

At Crater Rim Survey, morning light reveals pale cliffs, a quiet inner crater and a colony settled along the sheltered terraces. Sample trays wait beside the survey instrument; below, the greenhouse catches the same light as the distant basin. High above this ringed world, a dusty arc crosses the sky. Another ordinary day begins very far from Earth.
