Instrumental only, about five minutes. Use the attached reference for palette, tonal balance, harmonic character and spaciousness; compose a new piece in that sound, not a repeat of it.

Open morning synth ambient, beatless and free-time. Mood: a working geological observatory above a pale caldera on a ringed inhabited planet; patient curiosity, open space, modest belonging in an ancient landscape.

Start with the low-mid foundation and two middle-register voices already established. Around 1:00, add the airy layer and let the intervals begin to widen. Between 1:30 and 3:30, let the first voice ask its two-note question twice and the second answer each time with a different contour, while the harmony reveals a few warmer consonant notes. After 3:30, let the intervals settle back, keep the airy layer moving, and hold a stable, gently moving texture to the end. Even level throughout; no build, no heroic arrival, no final cadence, no fade to silence.

No vocals, drums, percussion, arpeggios, bells, sound effects, bass drops, risers or crescendos.
