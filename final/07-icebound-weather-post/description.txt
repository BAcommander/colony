The weather report is the colony's first message each morning.

Beyond the warm windows of Icebound Weather Post, a frozen sea meets cliffs of ancient blue ice. Enclosed passages link the station to three small habitats, keeping neighbours close through the long cold. Beneath a pale ringed moon, the instruments record another change on the horizon. Someone will read it before opening the outer door.

Welcome to Ambient Colony. Stay a while and listen.
