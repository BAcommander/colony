Instrumental only, about five minutes. Use the attached reference for palette, tonal balance, harmonic character and spaciousness; compose a new piece in that sound, not a repeat of it.

Spacious desert-night synth ambient, beatless and free-time. Mood: a small communications station above a dry salt basin on a rust-red world; patient listening, enormous distance, restrained melancholy with a thread of warmth.

Start with the broad pad, modest bass and the faint wind bed already established, and let the distant resonant voice give one early reply. Between 1:00 and 3:00, let that reply change its interval and move between two registers while the underlying chord turns gradually through open fifths and suspended colours. Around 3:00, thin one pad layer away, then bring it back with a slightly different voicing. After 4:00, hold a stable, gently moving texture with one last distant reply. Even level throughout; no build, no climax, no final cadence, no fade to silence.

No vocals, drums, percussion, arpeggios, sound effects, rhythmic panning, risers or climaxes.
