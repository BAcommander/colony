# 13 — Below the Snowline: ice-planet retheme

User direction, 2026-10-25: "could we retheme below the snow like to an ICE planet / star wars / Hoth ambients / inspiration, like what we did for the Tron one (11 that's WIP?)"

The Tron-inspired reference project is unnumbered Spirewatch; catalog 11 is The Empty Junction. Keep this scene at catalog 13. Below the Snowline remains the working public title; Hoth-inspired ice-planet ambience is the new creative direction.

## Visual direction

Retain the warm, believable monitoring room and broad pressure window. Rework the closed glacier vault into an ice-base vista at the glacier mouth: snow-buried occupied modules, insulated hangar access, a structurally anchored enclosed bridge, a frozen fissure, distant pale mountains and a stormy polar sky. Keep analog practical equipment and warm windows against blue-white snow. The clean source itself should establish the final lighting and visual appeal, following the Spirewatch lesson that the thumbnail and animation should share the same scene appearance.

This is an artwork candidate, not an approved replacement. Preserve the existing v2 source and approved thumbnail. If the new clean art is accepted, derive the next thumbnail from this actual plate rather than regenerating a visually different scene for marketing.

## Proposed animation after artwork review

Primary motion: readable windblown powder in the exposed ravine and distant basin, with separate depth, scale and speed; slow transport through the actual storm clouds. Begin with a short full-composition principal-motion test. Avoid a uniform snowfall overlay and keep all weather outside the room.

Supporting motion: a small anchored exhaust plume if the generated outlet supports one; independent warm-window holds and modest instrument telemetry. No moving bridge, wandering dish, flying ships, combat or foreground snow. The ice, structures, camera and room geometry remain fixed. The old meltwater/reflection plan no longer applies to a frozen fissure.

Read the Colony animation knowledge bank and Icebound Weather Post source-specific snow/mist lessons before implementation. Reuse accepted algorithms only after mapping new source-coordinate masks and occluders to this exact plate. Do not reuse the old cavern's water masks or the Tron scene's neon/traffic masks. Freeze the new source hash and list protected edges before any motion work.

Default target, subject to later user direction: genuine twenty-second silent 720p/30fps preview, three-repeat review, then matching 4K only after visual acceptance. Keep forward transport and matched state/velocity across the seam. Native source dimensions must be disclosed; output scaling is not new detail. No animation, audio generation, long assembly, commit or push is part of this artwork retheme.

Music direction for later preparation: spacious frozen-outpost ambience, distant weather and restrained warm electronic tones. Existing music prompts describe the older setting and should be revised after the new art direction is accepted.
