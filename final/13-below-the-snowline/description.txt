Take shelter at a remote ice outpost inspired by Hoth, the frozen world from Star Wars.

From the warm monitoring room, watch snow sweep across the ravine, the bridge lights glow and a shuttle pass above the base. Inside, the ventilation hums and the occasional radio call comes through between gusts.

Shelter on the Ice Plain and Outpost on the Ice are arranged into a continuous ambient soundtrack with wind, blowing snow, radio chatter, ventilation and the creak of the outpost. Settle in to read, work or wind down.

Music and artwork were created using AI tools. Animation, music arrangement and sound design were assembled for Ambient Colony.

Welcome to Ambient Colony. Stay a while and listen.
