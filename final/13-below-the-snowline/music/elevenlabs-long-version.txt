Compose instrumental cinematic electronic ambient for Below the Snowline, a warm monitoring room in a human colony beneath a vast blue ice vault on a frozen planet. An enclosed bridge joins inhabited rock ledges, while daylight filters down through the ice. The emotional centre is shelter, quiet companionship and enormous stillness beyond the glass. Beatless, free-time and unhurried.

Pair a close warm low-mid analog-style pad with a distant smooth upper-mid voice. Let each sustain and change independently: the warm layer may remain while the cool layer gently shifts an interval, then reverse that relationship. Use open fifths and suspended harmonies interspersed with small consonant colour changes; do not lock the entire piece to one drone note or one unchanging chord. A very soft bowed-metal-like synthesizer texture may enter occasionally, with no scraping attack, metallic strike or sharp resonance.

Keep the warm layer relatively dry and intimate, and place the cool layer farther away through a long, diffuse tail. Maintain an audible middle register, restrained bass, smooth high end and comfortable stable intensity. Cold distance must not become menace or shrillness. No vocals, choir-like formants, drums, percussion, crystalline bells, repeated arpeggios, wind recordings, ice cracks, water effects, alarm tones, impacts or dramatic rises. Avoid an ominous sub drone. Leave enough quiet space to feel the scale without losing gentle harmonic life.

For the longer piece, aim for approximately five minutes if the interface supports it. Use the selected own short test as a reference for feel, palette and intensity, not as a request for an exact continuation. Develop its independent voices through a few gentle changes of voicing and density, then return toward the opening texture without an obvious reprise. Avoid repeating a short phrase every minute, long empty introductions, a climax or a final cadence. Preserve a useful sustained texture near both ends for later local loop-point selection and listening review; seamlessness will be checked after export.
