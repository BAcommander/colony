The next train is hours away, but the junction stays lit.

At The Empty Junction, a remote alien colony keeps its freight route open between pale mineral spires. Warm windows trace the passage from the signal cabin to the homes above the yard. Beyond the rails, the pass disappears into the evening haze. For this shift, there is nowhere else you need to be.
