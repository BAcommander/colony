Original instrumental ambient score inspired by Warhammer 40,000, for a quiet vigil on a fortress-monastery terrace beneath the crimson skies of Baal. Ancient stone, distant desert and one warm lantern. Carry the weight of duty and the tenderness of a place that still feels like home.

Start with two or three exposed low-mid voices, gently bowed in colour, with a rounded analogue tone close to the listener. Let one inner note shift and another voice emerge from the distance. Build into a wider sustained harmony with a long, stone-room decay, then let different layers recede at different speeds. Allow stretches with only a few audible tones. When a familiar chord colour returns, change its voicing, register or depth.

A short, soft piano fragment may surface occasionally and dissolve into the sustained tones. Give it long, irregular rests. Let the music alternate between intimacy and scale with smooth entrances, restrained bass and clear middle detail. Keep enough contrast to stay interesting over a long listen. Begin inside the atmosphere and end on an open, audible texture.

Use original musical material. No vocals, choir, beat, march, recurring arpeggio, fanfare, abrupt peaks, battle effects or environmental sound effects.
