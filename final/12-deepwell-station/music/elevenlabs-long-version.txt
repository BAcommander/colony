Compose instrumental cinematic electronic ambient for Deepwell Station, a sheltered water-reclamation gallery looking across pale mineral terraces and blue-grey brine toward cliffside homes on another planet. Convey clear daylight, dependable work and slow replenishment. Keep it calm, spacious and gently hopeful, with no beat or fixed metre.

Use broad, rounded midrange pads with two or three independent sustained voices that overlap at unequal intervals. Let voicings subtly widen and narrow, passing a soft inner note from one layer to another rather than moving the whole chord block at once. A subdued felt-key-like synthesizer may introduce an occasional small descending phrase; leave long spaces around it and vary its notes. Add a thin, smooth upper layer that catches the harmony like reflected daylight, without bright glints, bells or sparkling arpeggios.

The low register should provide quiet support while the middle carries the musical interest. Use small audible harmonic changes, restrained stereo movement and diffuse reverb that leaves the voices distinguishable. Keep the perceived level even and the high frequencies soft. No voices, choir pads, percussion, drums, rhythmic pumping, fixed ostinatos, literal water sounds, bubbling, dripping, mechanical noises or interface beeps. Avoid a static single-note bass drone, sudden filter sweeps and climactic swells. The piece should feel replenishing without becoming sentimental or melodically busy.

For the longer piece, aim for approximately five minutes if the interface supports it. Use the selected own short test as a reference for feel, palette and intensity, not as a request for an exact continuation. Develop its independent voices through a few gentle changes of voicing and density, then return toward the opening texture without an obvious reprise. Avoid repeating a short phrase every minute, long empty introductions, a climax or a final cadence. Preserve a useful sustained texture near both ends for later local loop-point selection and listening review; seamlessness will be checked after export.
