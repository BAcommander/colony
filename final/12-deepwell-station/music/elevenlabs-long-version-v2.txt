Instrumental only, about five minutes. Use the attached reference for palette, tonal balance, harmonic character and spaciousness; compose a new piece in that sound, not a repeat of it.

Calm daylight synth ambient, beatless and free-time. Mood: a sheltered waterworks gallery on a colony world, clear morning light on pale terraces and brine; dependable, replenishing, quietly hopeful.

Start with the broad midrange pads already established, two voices overlapping. Around 1:00, add the third voice and begin passing single inner notes between the layers. Between 1:30 and 3:30, let the felt-piano tone play three or four small descending figures, each different and surrounded by space, while the thin upper layer follows the harmony like reflected light. After 3:30, thin back toward the two opening voices with one last figure, and hold a stable, gently moving texture to the end. Even level throughout; no build, no climax, no final cadence, no fade to silence.

No vocals, drums, percussion, arpeggios, sound effects, filter sweeps or swells to a climax.
