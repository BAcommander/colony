# Ambient Colony - descriptions and search tags

## Shared voice

Remote places that still feel inhabited: warm windows, machinery working, and someone keeping the lights on. Give each location one small story and a little mystery. Use original scene-specific prose, not competitor lore. Keep the tone welcoming and cinematic.

Aim for roughly 50-90 words before hashtags. Lead with a memorable setting-specific sentence, followed by two or three sentences rooted in the scene. The current three descriptions preserve the brainstormed copy, with the user's chosen closing line:

**Welcome to Ambient Colony. Stay a while and listen.**

End with three directly relevant hashtags: two topic tags and `#AmbientColony`. This is our house convention, not an algorithmic ranking guarantee. Keep hashtags distinct from Studio search tags.

## Files per release

- `description.txt`: ready-to-copy description body, including the sign-off and hashtags.
- `youtube-tags.txt`: one comma-separated line for the YouTube Studio Tags field, without hashtags. Each video's list must be strictly below 500 characters, counting commas and spaces. Keep ample spare capacity for platform counting differences.
- `manifest.json`: metadata filenames, SHA-256 hashes, tag counts and character counts.

Use relevant scene names, topic phrases and natural spelling variants such as ambience/ambiance. Avoid competitor brands, unrelated popular searches, unsupported duration claims and unverified soundtrack claims. These are suggested relevance-based tags, not search-volume-tested keywords. Do not paste the comma-separated tag list into the description.

## Audio status

The current exported masters are silent loops. These descriptions establish the narrative for publication; they do not claim a finished soundtrack. Once audio is chosen, add one accurate sentence about the actual music and intended listening use before the shared sign-off, and update tags if appropriate. Do not claim human-composed, no AI music, binaural audio, particular frequencies or hours of runtime unless verified for the delivered upload.

## Guidance checked 2026-09-25

- [YouTube description guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en): make each description unique, put useful information early, and use one or two relevant main terms naturally in title and description.
- [YouTube hashtag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en): use directly related hashtags; up to three may appear by the title.
- [YouTube search-tag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en): titles, thumbnails and descriptions matter more for discovery; search tags generally play a small role and can help with misspellings. Keep effort focused on the actual content and presentation.

The 500-character requirement here is the user's requested per-video limit. Counts in manifests exclude the trailing file newline; the saved lists also pass with that newline included.
