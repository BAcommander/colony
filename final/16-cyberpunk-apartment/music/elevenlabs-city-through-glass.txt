Original instrumental ambient score for a rain-soaked future skyline seen from inside the same warmly lit apartment. A companion passage to Amber Room. The city feels wide and distant, with a little warmth still close to the listener.

Use rounded analogue synth voices, a faint bowed grain and a warm low-mid foundation. Leave more space between the voices and let a cool, soft harmonic sheen emerge from sustained tones. Let the distant harmony gradually fill out as the close layer recedes. Spend time in both exposed and fuller textures. Stagger their entrances and decays so the whole mix does not rise and fall together. Later, let a warmer chord colour return in a new voicing with a lighter texture around it.

Keep everything free-time and quietly expressive. If a melodic fragment appears, let it pass and leave a long gap before another. Keep the highest texture soft and distant. Begin with audible atmosphere and finish suspended in a sustained texture that could meet the warmer companion passage.

No vocals, beat, kick, sequenced bass, repeating arpeggio, recurring hook, bright bell attacks, build or environmental sound effects.
