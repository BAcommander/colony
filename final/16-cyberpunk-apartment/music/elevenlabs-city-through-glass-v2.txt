Original instrumental ambient exploration score for the vast rainy future skyline seen from inside a warmly lit apartment. A companion to Amber Room, with more distance, wonder and a trace of unresolved longing. The city feels immense while a little human warmth remains close to the listener.

Let broad harmonies slowly open beyond the room. Move between exposed voices and a wider sustained texture, allowing a small tension to gather before a warmer harmony releases it. Stagger the entrances and decays so different depths remain audible. After a fuller passage, leave space for its quieter answer; let later returns change voicing, register and emotional colour.

Give the score occasional connected melodic fragments that grow from its inner voices. Let a phrase become briefly clear, dissolve into the distance and return in a changed form after an unequal pause. Preserve a sense of development across the quiet stretches rather than settling into one unchanging chord.

Keep the palette coherent: rounded analogue synth voices, a faint bowed grain, warm lower support and a cool, soft upper sheen. Allow the distant layer to expand while the near layer thins, then gradually bring the room's warmth back into focus. Retain expressive swells and retreat, smooth entrances and controlled peaks, without a dramatic climax. Begin with the atmosphere present and end open on an audible texture that can meet the warmer companion passage.

No vocals, beat, sequenced bass, repeating arpeggios, abrasive high attacks, heroic fanfares or environmental sound effects.
