Original instrumental ambient score for a warm apartment high above a rain-soaked future city at night. An amber lamp, a quiet room and a vast blue skyline through the glass. Intimate, unhurried and faintly wistful.

Start inside the sound with a few soft, rounded analogue synth voices and a warm low-mid foundation. Give the sustained tones a slight bowed grain. Let the harmony gradually gather weight as one inner voice changes and another drifts in. Hold the fuller texture, then let the layers fall away at different speeds until only a few tones remain. When familiar chords return, give them a different voicing, register or distance. Leave the quiet stretches unequal in length.

A single softened electric-piano note or small fragment can surface and decay into the pads. Keep these gestures occasional and irregular, with long passages of sustained harmony alone. Keep the bass soft, the high end subdued and the changes gentle enough to work under concentration. End on an open, audible sustained texture.

No vocals, beat, kick, sequenced bass, repeating arpeggio, recurring hook, bright bell attacks, build or environmental sound effects.
