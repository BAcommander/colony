## What can and cannot be inferred

The reported averages differ by about 48%, but this is a sequential before/after
observation. The pre-existing decline, elapsed time, impressions, traffic source,
audience composition and viewing behaviour are not controlled here. CTR,
impression counts and watch-time evidence are not shown. It cannot establish
that text caused a 48% reduction, that clean art universally wins, or that our
approved branded thumbnails should be replaced.

Design hypothesis: removing typography can give the scene a single visual focus;
a large heading introduces a competing high-contrast element. Tiny supporting
copy may add little at phone size. These are visual interpretations to test,
not measured explanations of this particular performance change.

For Ambient Colony, compare the same scene/crop/brightness with (1) current
approved branding, (2) short title only, and (3) scene only. Hold the video title
steady if testing thumbnail text. Preview at small sizes and run a concurrent
YouTube test if the account/video is eligible. Assess the platform's watch-time
result together with impressions, CTR and traffic context. Keep inconclusive
results inconclusive; do not silently revise the approved identity.
