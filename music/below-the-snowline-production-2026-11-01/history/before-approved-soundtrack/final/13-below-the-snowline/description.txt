Above the vault, the frozen world stretches for days. Down here, the lights are warm.

Below the Snowline follows a sheltered colony built into the rock beneath an immense blue ice ceiling. An enclosed bridge carries the daily route between homes, while a narrow meltwater channel runs through the gorge below. From the monitoring room, daylight arrives through the ice like a message from somewhere very far away.
