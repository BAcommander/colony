# Crater Rim Survey first motion review

[Watch the eight-second clouds and haze preview](Crater-Rim-Survey-clouds-and-haze-v1-8s.mp4). The camera stays fixed. The central cloud bank drifts right above the caldera. A slower, warm veil crosses the distant floor while faster, low haze moves across the middle basin behind the near ridges. [Clouds only](Crater-Rim-Survey-clouds-isolated-v1-8s.mp4) · [Haze only](Crater-Rim-Survey-haze-isolated-v1-8s.mp4)

The [source-space scene plan](scene-plan.json) freezes the v2 artwork and traces safe sky runs, window apertures and the basin boundary. A continuous horizontal warp only samples clear sky, using the Snowline approach to prevent copies of protected silhouettes. The two haze depths reuse the shared Saltline gust transport and take their speeds and routes from the Crater Rim plan. The near ledges and window frames occlude the haze.

All three silent 720p/30 fps clips decoded to 240 frames each with sequential timestamps. Pixels outside the effect masks matched the clean source in every frame before resizing and encoding. Three decoded cloud patches travelled 31 preview pixels right in 7.75 seconds, with template correlations from 0.988 to 0.995. At three sampled times, the combined clip lifted at least five luma codes across roughly 12,400–13,500 basin pixels compared with the cloud-only clip. Decoded stills were inspected. Playback review is pending. This is a continuous motion test; its endpoints have yet to be designed as a loop. [Delivery checks](delivery.json)

Reproduce with `python music/crater-rim-animation-v1/render_preview.py` from a checkout with the output paths free, using the existing NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV and imageio-ffmpeg environment. After visual feedback, build a 20-second periodic candidate and check the encoded seam before committing to a longer render. For the later sound audition, use the enclosed survey-room perspective in the [shared sound workflow](../../creative/MUSIC_WORKFLOW.md#design-weather-and-sound-together).
