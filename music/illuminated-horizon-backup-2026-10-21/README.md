## Durable lessons

Source separation exposes existing material; it cannot compose a missing middle.
If an arpeggio carries most harmony and melody, even perfect subtraction may
leave little useful music. Listen to a short proof before engineering an app.
Do not normalize nearly empty remainders independently and describe them as a
successful breakdown. A working model/runtime or a passing signal test does not
establish musical usefulness. Assistant checks here were technical, not listening.

Audio reference carries groove/instrumentation as well as palette; instructions
excluding arpeggios do not ensure selective transfer. For a contrasting insert,
try text-led generation without the pulse-heavy reference, then check harmony.
