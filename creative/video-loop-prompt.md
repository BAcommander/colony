## Image-to-video prompt

Use the supplied concept image as the exact visual anchor. Create a ten-second cinematic photorealistic ambient shot intended for seamless continuous repetition behind sleep, focus, and study music. Preserve the composition, architecture, equipment, materials, landscape, and lighting of the image.

The camera is completely locked on a tripod for the entire shot: no pan, tilt, zoom, dolly, handheld movement, reframing, focus pull, or parallax. All solid objects remain fixed and retain their exact shapes. Keep the scene quiet, sheltered, and gently inhabited without showing people.

Animate only [ONE PRIMARY ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECT] and optionally [ONE VERY SUBTLE SECONDARY EFFECT]. Motion must be slow, low amplitude, and spatially localized. Preserve fine detail and stable edges throughout. Maintain constant exposure, white balance, shadows, weather intensity, and time of day. Lamps remain steadily illuminated; instrument displays retain their layout.

Design all animated motion as a continuous periodic cycle of exactly ten seconds. The state at t=10 seconds must match t=0, including movement direction and speed, so the transition has no visible jump or pause. No fade-in, fade-out, cuts, scene transitions, new objects, dramatic events, or accelerating action. Do not reverse falling precipitation or use an obvious forward/reverse effect.

Landscape 16:9. Request the highest native quality supported by the selected video generator. Natural physically plausible light, detailed weathered materials, restrained cinematic grading, clear shadow detail, no artificial sharpening. Visuals only; no generated music, speech, captions, logos, or watermarks.

## Render checks

Watch at least three repetitions. Check the seam for position, brightness, and velocity discontinuities. Check window frames, antennas, monitor geometry, and distant horizons for warping. Remove any duplicate endpoint frame if it causes a pause. Precipitation and drifting particles may need a composited loop or a carefully masked transition; request fewer effects if the render is unstable.
