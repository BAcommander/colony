## Complete generation prompt

A compact food-propagation and seed nursery within an inhabited colony at the edge of an alien broadleaf biome. Camera looks obliquely along a waist-high growing bench toward a tall rectangular weather window. The near bench holds two trays of modest recognizable food seedlings, a hand-watering vessel with a single spout, folded gloves and a closed seed box. A task chair and a simple environmental-control panel are enough. Leave a clear aisle; keep irrigation away from electrical housings.

Outside, the native canopy has thick dark stems with whorls of broad elongated leathery fronds. Each frond has a translucent amber margin and opaque olive-grey centre; low daylight passing through the thin margins gives a warm transmitted edge, not bioluminescence. Leaves are attached through plausible petioles and supported by a coherent branching system. Near leaves show age, torn edges and variation; distant crowns overlap in quiet large masses. No Earth palms, giant flowers, fungal caps or unrelated alien species mixed together.

A sheltered colony courtyard sits in a deliberate clearing between root zones. Low insulated homes, a shared kitchen entrance and the next growing room connect under a simple covered route. Root barriers and a perimeter drainage channel explain how buildings coexist with the wet biome. Warm windows and a neatly hung work apron establish daily life without people. The cultivated food plants inside are visually different from the native vegetation outside, making the purpose of the room clear.

Use a standing 40 mm perspective, with the bench confined to the near left and the courtyard visible beyond the right half of the glass. Soft cloud-filtered daylight lights the native amber leaf margins; neutral-white horticultural fixtures illuminate the crop trays without a pink grow-light wash. A small warm desk lamp may light the seed box. Maintain dry clean working surfaces, modest condensation only near the cooler outer glass edge, and quiet realistic materials.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.

## Review before selection

Keep native/cultivated plants distinct, transmitted light physically localized and paths clear. Avoid glowing foliage, giant Earth plants and nursery clutter.
