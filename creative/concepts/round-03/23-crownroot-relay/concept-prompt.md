## Complete generation prompt

An exterior view of a low communications and route-monitoring station at the edge of an inhabited forest colony. One weatherproof rectangular building sits on a raised dry slab, with a recessed warm entrance, two modest antenna masts and one orderly battery cabinet. A sheltered pedestrian route disappears behind it toward two occupied research homes, visible through gaps in the growth. A low maintenance gate opens onto a usable path; antennas have a clear patch of sky rather than being tangled in branches.

The native forest has one repeating botanical architecture: thick tapering grey stems divide near the crown into three or four load-bearing arms, each supporting broad upward-curved fan plates with ribbed undersides. The plates overlap like irregular shallow roofs but are not circular mushroom caps. Older plants have fractured edges and lower collapsed fans; smaller juveniles repeat the same structure. Roots form broad grounded buttresses with dark moist soil between them. Muted petrol-green upper surfaces and copper-grey undersides make the near forms unfamiliar; distant plants lose detail into olive-grey mist. No Earth conifers, palms, bioluminescence or mixed fantasy flora.

Construction is set back from root systems, with culverts beneath the path and generous clearance around the building. Cool overcast daylight filters through the fan canopy; warm doorway and handrail fixtures show a safe inhabited route. The nearest fan edge frames the upper view without concealing the station. Camera at normal standing height, 50 mm, on a dry service track. The station occupies the lower third, while the layered canopy conveys scale without turning the scene into a gigantic tree-city. Keep ordinary bolts, doors and handrails legible, with wetness confined to weather-exposed surfaces.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.
