## Complete generation prompt

A weather-sealed tram shelter at the edge of a residential district on an alien world whose ancient high-temperature surface has weathered into broad dark glassy mineral slabs. The camera is seated inside the small station beside a plain bench, facing a wide window across two tram rails on a concrete-supported track bed. A short platform has a covered connection back to nearby occupied houses at the frame edge. No train is present; this is the quiet interval between shifts.

Beyond the platform, large uneven slabs extend across a shallow plain. Their surfaces are mostly matte smoky green-black, with restrained reflective streaks only on fresh fracture faces and shallow rain films. Pale mineral seams fill irregular cracks; rounded edges and accumulated dust make the terrain old and natural. There are no crystal spires, luminous fissures, mirror-perfect sheets or uniformly tiled polygons. A few low ribs of translucent mineral catch the distant sky at grazing angles, secondary to the broad ground shapes.

The rails run on a raised insulated foundation above the fragile slab surface, with drainage and simple maintenance access. At the far side of the plain a compact colony interchange is visible through a thin grey rain curtain. Its large warm entrance and connected low rooflines are a clear destination for the tram. A single coherent atmospheric front moves across the horizon; no added celestial bodies.

Camera: natural seated 50 mm perspective, bench end and one grounded timetable-display casing in the near foreground; the display contains only indistinct blocks, no readable invented text. A sealed coat locker and a small handrail complete the shelter. Soft rainy daylight is cool and neutral. One amber ceiling fixture warms the seating area, with sensible spill on the floor and little reflection on the glass. Human comfort comes from a dry protected waiting place and a route home, not decorative futuristic machinery.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.

## Review before selection

Check track gauge/foundations, platform access and material restraint. The glassplain must read as weathered mineral geology rather than shiny CGI tiles or lava.

## Later motion proposal — not a render request

Rain on the distant plain and subdued reflection movement in shallow films; fixed rails, slabs and shelter. No train or moving focal object required.
