## Complete generation prompt

A small communal dining room serving a colony on the habitable twilight belt of a synchronously rotating planet. Look from beside a real table toward a broad insulated window. In the foreground are two plain chairs, one cleared meal tray, a closed book and a coat hung on a hook. At the side, a compact service counter has an ordinary water dispenser. The communal space is modest and used; leave tables and walking routes mostly empty.

Outside, the nearest occupied homes are low rounded insulated structures partly banked with pale local aggregate. Enclosed pedestrian links join them to a shared food-growing house. Beyond the district, the land transitions from dusty plum-grey ground into a broad white frost margin, with low sinuous wind-carved ridges extending toward blue darkness. This is a continuous environmental transition, not a razor-straight artificial line. Low dense native ground cover forms dull overlapping silver scales in the sheltered unfrozen pockets; no familiar grass, trees or giant mushrooms. Frost baffles and drift-free raised doorways visibly respond to the climate.

The low sun is just outside frame toward the left, supplying a narrow band of warm horizon light and long coherent shadows. Cold scattered sky light comes from the darker right-hand landscape; no second sun or moon. Interior ceiling lamps provide soft warm-white light, with one modest blue service indicator. The room feels like the place neighbours gather after a shift, not a lonely military control room. A seated 40 mm perspective keeps furniture human-sized and leaves the warm settlement readable beneath the huge quiet sky.
