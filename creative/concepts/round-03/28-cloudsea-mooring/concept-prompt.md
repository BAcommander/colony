## Complete generation prompt

Inside the maintenance lounge of an inhabited aerostat colony floating in a dense, breathable atmospheric layer of a fictional temperate world. The structure is supported by enormous buoyant envelopes, not antigravity. A limited visible section of the near envelope curves above the upper-left window edge, with tension cables attaching to a clearly load-bearing truss outside. Show these supports as part of the composition rather than tiny decoration.

Through broad segmented windows, a second residential gondola hangs beneath its own partly visible pale envelope, connected to the near structure by a short articulated enclosed gangway. Its bearings, flexible joint and service loops allow small relative movement; no kilometre-long rigid bridge between free balloons. A few warm windows identify occupied communal spaces. No separate aircraft, spacecraft, floating rocks or unsupported platforms.

Below and beyond the colony lies a huge pale ochre cloud deck broken into broad cells, with smooth slate-violet shadow troughs between them. A higher thin cloud layer screens the sun; there is no ground or Earth coastline in sight, no rings and no extra planets. The unfamiliar atmospheric palette is muted and belongs to the cloud material and light rather than a saturated colour filter. At different distances the cells vary naturally in size and contrast.

The near room has a bolted fabric bench, a restrained weather display, one secured service notebook and a small latched cabinet. Use a natural 35 mm view from seated height, with a low window sill and visible floor so the room feels usable. Warm indirect task lighting touches the bench; diffuse cloud-reflected daylight lights the window frame. Matte off-white external fabric, dark rubber joints and slightly scuffed internal blue-grey panels remain distinct. This is a calm communal workplace suspended in a vast atmosphere, not a luxury observation deck or a dangerous open balcony.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.

## Review before selection

The speculative engineering must still communicate lift, load paths and articulation. Reject floating structures without envelopes/supports; avoid tiny balloons supporting impossibly large habitats.

## Later motion proposal — not a render request

Slow broad cloud transport behind fixed gondola masks. No default drifting camera, swinging bridge or deforming envelope; loop is a calm stylized observation, not flight simulation.
