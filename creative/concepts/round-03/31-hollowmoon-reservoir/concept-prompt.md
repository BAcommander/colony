## Complete generation prompt

A quiet water-storage gallery inside the sealed excavation of an airless small moon. This is an engineered inhabited interior: air and warmth are maintained inside, while the exterior is vacuum. The viewpoint is on a dry upper walkway beside a long rectangular covered reservoir with one glazed inspection section; its still dark water is visible through that section. A simple filtration manifold and accessible isolation valves occupy one side bay, separated from protected electrical cabinets.

At the far end of the excavation, a circular pressure-rated viewing wall is divided into manageable glass sectors by deep radial ribs. It reveals a close charcoal-and-pale lunar ridge against black space. The ridge shows broad exposed layers and old impact breccia, with sharp sunlit edges and dark unsoftened shadows; no atmospheric haze, weather clouds or blowing dust outside. On the ridge, an insulated enclosed transfer tube leads from the excavation into two small buried residential entrances with modest lit vestibules. The connected settlement is attached to rock with identifiable foundations and sealed joints.

The interior excavation has load-bearing rock pillars, a lined ceiling and visible structural frames around the aperture. A short flight of stairs connects the gallery to a lower dry maintenance aisle, with complete landings and handrails. Warm work lamps reflect faintly in the reservoir inspection glass; a few restrained blue service lights locate the lower route. A plain bench, one closed maintenance book and a stored hose on a proper reel suggest the daily care of a shared water supply. No indoor lake lapping against live equipment.

Use a level 35 mm view from the gallery entrance, giving the water-storage system, pressure window and distant residential route clear depth. The moon's white sunlight contributes a bounded patch near the far window; the occupied interior is primarily lit by its own fixtures. A sparse dark starfield may appear only in unlit sky, without bright nebulae or an oversized parent planet. No people, spacecraft or magically unsupported excavation roof.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.
