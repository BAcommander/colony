## Complete generation prompt

A modest municipal records room high in an established off-world colony, showing that the settlement has grown beyond its first modules. Inside, one desk has a compact document scanner, two dim archive displays, a closed binder and a fabric office chair. A shallow shelf holds a small number of plain record boxes. Maintain tidy working wear, a clean window and reachable equipment; no ruined computers, paper mountains or hanging cable tangles.

The broad angled window looks over a city terraced into immense pale claystone shelves. Buildings are compact ceramic-clad courtyard blocks with rounded corners and deep wind hoods, stacked only where grounded retaining structures support them. Enclosed tram routes follow the terrace edges and connect neighbourhoods; planted food-growing roofs and shared service courts help the city read as lived in. A few slender utility towers emerge above the lower blocks, but there are no recognizable Earth landmarks, glass needle skyscrapers or flying traffic.

Beyond the city, the planet's erosion has left a continuous line of broad scalloped pale ridges above an iron-grey dry basin. A long low front of fine suspended mineral haze sits against their bases, leaving the scalloped upper silhouettes readable. Glazing and façades face away from that dusty prevailing wind; protected courtyard windows remain warm. No moon is needed. The claystone landform, coherent ceramic construction and connected terraced districts establish the unfamiliar world together.

Use a seated 45 mm perspective from behind the corner of the archive desk. Interior takes roughly the left/lower third; the city holds the middle distance with a clear hierarchy of blocks, routes and neighbourhoods. Neutral late-afternoon light catches the pale façades; one warm desk lamp gives the room a modest refuge. Distant urban detail softens naturally instead of remaining equally sharp to the horizon. The tone is continuity and ordinary civic life, not a dead city or dystopian spectacle.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.

## Review before selection

Check real neighbourhood routes and hillside supports. City scale should be large but readable; avoid dense texture noise, copied skyline silhouettes and identical repeated building stamps.
