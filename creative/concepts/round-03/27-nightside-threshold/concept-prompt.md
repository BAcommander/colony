## Complete generation prompt

An occupied colony service passage leads toward a heavy sealed observation vestibule on the permanent night side of a habitable-belt planet. The camera is inside the warm section. Curved insulated metal lining, short tidy utility channels, two service doors and a straight accessible walkway lead to the end window. A single short stair connects a lower maintenance landing to the main level; all landings and handrails meet. One small bench with a folded insulated work coat suggests a returning shift. No people.

Beyond the reinforced end glass, shallow glacial shelves form broad interlocking terraces under a deep desaturated violet-black sky. Cold haze partly hides a nearby low enclosed service link leading to another lit habitat entrance. The exterior ground is matte blue-grey frost with wind combs, not fluffy indoor snow. A faint broad curtain of muted green auroral light is confined high in the atmosphere and partly screened by thin cloud. It is subtle and continuous, without sharp laser stripes or a blazing nebula. Nearby electrical fixtures, not the aurora, illuminate the walkway and doors.

The threshold shows the thermal transition clearly: the inner walkway is dry; frost is confined to the outside of seals and unheated external recesses. Pressure doors, gaskets and wall penetrations make sense. Exterior rails and entrance canopies are low and wind-resistant, with a sheltered path rather than an exposed open balcony. No snowstorm inside the heated passage.

Natural 40 mm eye-height composition with the vestibule offset slightly right. Warm-white lights illuminate the inner bench and service door; short cool-blue fixtures guide the final few metres to the lock. Keep local light falloff and readable darkness between pools. The exterior reveals enough terrain and connected habitation to locate the colony, while the near space remains the main refuge. No giant planet or extra moon.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.
