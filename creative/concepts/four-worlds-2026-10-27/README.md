## Learning

Retaining source-specific combinations of hardware era, construction, light and scale gives these outputs a clearer intended influence than the first abstract source-first studies, in assistant judgment only. The user has not yet confirmed that the vibe is successful. More machinery is not automatically a stronger thumbnail: quiet large forms in C1 contrast with dense B3/D3. Emotional short headlines make an alternative to unfamiliar destination names visible for comparison, but text can still occupy a large part of the picture. Selection and eventual audience testing remain necessary.
