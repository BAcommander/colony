## Complete generation prompt

Create a quiet freight interchange on the cold edge of the habitable twilight belt of a synchronously rotating alien planet. The railway carries quarried ceramic feedstock toward a compact occupied processing district. The immediate subject is a low oval-ended, ribbed metal signal cabin with a warm narrow window, weather-sealed doorway and short supported stair, standing beside one intelligible track turnout. Show only two routes after the turnout. Keep rails paired, gauge consistent, sleepers properly seated and switches mechanically understandable; no web of crossing rails or decorative rail fragments.

The environmental signature is a procession of enormous pale limestone-like mineral fins whose eroded, perforated crests form uneven comb silhouettes across the middle distance. They grow out of continuous violet-grey sedimentary beds, not isolated floating monuments. Bases are thick and weight-bearing, crest openings rounded by erosion; irregular spacing and broken fins prevent a repeated pattern. A thin band of pale apricot daylight lies along the distant horizon while the upper sky remains steel blue. The sheltered cold-facing sides of the cuttings carry dull frost; the dry service path remains clear. No familiar forest, dark basalt rubble field or additional moon.

The track curves through an excavated low pass to a partly recessed freight hall with several warm clerestory windows. A protected pedestrian gallery connects that hall to occupied quarters built into the sheltered face of the mineral ridge. Freight bays, pedestrian doors and utilities occupy separate readable routes. Wind baffles and low insulated cabinets explain operation in the cold dusty cutting. A single supported service trunk follows the retaining wall; it never cuts through the track.

Camera: standing on the cabin's service platform, looking obliquely past its left-hand wall toward the junction, natural 40 mm perspective. A foreground handrail is a short framing element, not a fence across the image. The cabin and turnout fill the lower-left and centre; the mineral pass and settlement read beyond them. Diffuse twilight explains the cool scene, supported by two modest amber work lamps with local spill. One closed maintenance case beside the doorway suggests an ordinary ongoing shift. Keep the sky and part of the cutting quiet.

Render one original landscape 16:9 photographic location still for Ambient Colony, without lettering. This is an inhabited human colony on an unfamiliar world, maintained and working, with nobody visible. Use the described camera position and consistent human scale. Let supports, doorways, seals, maintenance access and service routes explain how the place works. Place only the few props specified; leave other surfaces quiet.

Keep painted metal, rubber, concrete, fabric and glass visually distinct, with modest wear at contact or weather-exposed areas. Preserve natural shadows, soft highlight roll-off and atmospheric loss of distant detail. The unusual environment should look observed through a real lens, with irregular natural structure rather than uniformly sharp procedural texture. No plastic gloss, exaggerated HDR, sharpening halos, duplicated props, warped furniture, decorative machinery or arbitrary extra cables. No people, spacecraft flybys, holograms, lettering, branding, fictional insignia, watermark, screenshot controls or borders. If reference images are supplied, use only their lighting, human scale and framing principles; do not copy their scene, Earth geography, titles or logos.

## Review before selection

Mineral fins, frost distribution and protective colony routes must still distinguish the world with no moon. Check turnout geometry and walking access. The cabin must feel engineered for this location rather than a wooden Earth signal box.
