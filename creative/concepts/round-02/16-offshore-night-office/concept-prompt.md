## Complete generation prompt

Create one original landscape 16:9 location photograph for Ambient Colony: Offshore Night Office, the maintenance room of an inhabited marine-energy district on a distant ocean planet. This world has a substantial atmosphere, cool liquid seas and a mostly drowned volcanic shelf. Show useful infrastructure visibly connected to human homes adapted to that world. No text or graphics.

Use a natural stationary 40 mm full-frame-equivalent view from the back-right of the modest office. The main window spans the centre and left. A sturdy lower-right workbench holds two dim instrumentation displays, a handheld diagnostic meter in a fitted tray and a closed maintenance log. One ordinary chair and a plain locker complete the room. Leave useful uncluttered surfaces. A modest warm under-shelf work light illuminates the bench. Thick window seals, sensible storm-shutter tracks and quiet insulated panels suggest a protected offshore workspace, without a wall of decorative CRTs or spacecraft-cockpit styling.

Outside, an enclosed service bridge begins at the office platform and leads to exactly three inhabited platform modules in a shallow curve across the middle distance. Rounded rectangular insulated shells have small warm windows, sealed entrance vestibules and protected maintenance doors. Enclosed bridges join actual doorway faces through supported bearings and flexible sealed collars. These are fixed platforms on broad piled caissons in a shallow submerged shelf, not floating modules inexplicably joined by rigid bridges. Show enough support structure, waterline staining and secondary maintenance access to explain the engineering without a forest of scaffolding. Protected power and thermal lines follow the same service route. Small entrance lamps mark the way between homes.

Two modest realistic offshore wind turbines stand beyond the habitation district on separate supported foundations, part of the marine-energy outpost. Each has one coherent rotor and practical service access. Keep turbines secondary to the homes, with silhouettes clear of celestial features. No impossible blades, futuristic giant rotors or crowded oil-rig machinery.

Give the planet an unfamiliar but coherent ocean and horizon. Water is subdued petrol-blue with a slight mineral-green cast near the shelf, forming long low swells and broken soft reflections rather than mirror strips. Low broad eroded volcanic atolls interrupt the far horizon: stepped pale ash-grey shelves over darker submerged bases, with worn scalloped edges and atmospheric softness. They are remnants of an ancient drowned volcanic landscape, not tall fantasy towers, floating rocks or familiar pointed sea stacks. Mineral variation is subtle and irregular, never bright pink stripes or evenly repeated bands.

Cloudy dusk has a restrained ash-violet upper sky and a thin pale silver-green clearing near the horizon. In the clearing show two small natural satellites separated by a few apparent diameters: a softly shaded pale crescent about three percent of frame width and a much smaller dim irregular moon about one percent. Their illuminated sides agree with the same off-frame sunset direction. Both are low contrast through atmospheric haze and clear of turbine blades. No rings, huge planets, dense starfield or extra celestial bodies. The water, drowned mineral shelf and inhabited infrastructure should still convey an alien world if the moons are overlooked.

Finish the clean artwork with inviting photographic depth: a warm dry bench in the foreground, readable connected habitats over cool water, quieter turbines and atolls farther away. Diffuse skylight and one warm work light explain the exposure. Preserve natural shadows, bounded window highlights, matte weatherproof coatings, faint glass reflections and restrained metal sheen. Sea-weather staining belongs at splash zones and exposed seams, not uniformly on every panel. Maintain coherent exterior geography and water level across the panes, plus clear structure boundaries and open water/sky regions for later animation.

The mood is the quiet night shift keeping an ocean colony supplied with power: far from Earth but connected to neighbours. No people, creatures, spacecraft, holograms, glowing oceans, neon colour, oversized celestial spectacle, excessive clutter, warped supports, miniature scale, plastic gloss, extreme HDR, sharpening halos, lettering, branding, watermark, UI or border. Output one complete clean cinematic photograph, not a collage or poster.
