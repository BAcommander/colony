## Complete generation prompt

Create one original landscape 16:9 location photograph for Ambient Colony: Rainline Relay, an elevated communications room serving a small human research colony in the flooded forest of a distant habitable planet. The world has rain, an atmosphere, liquid water and its own coherent native ecology. Its identity must be apparent in the environment and how people live there. No text or graphics.

Photograph from a standing-height back corner with a natural 40 mm full-frame-equivalent perspective. A compact ordinary radio desk occupies the lower-left edge: a supported radio transceiver, one subdued instrument display, a closed waterproof field notebook and one well-used chair. One modest warm desk lamp lights a useful clear surface. Broad sealed windows reveal the woodland and the route to neighbouring habitats. Use a fresh room layout, not the Floodplain control room again. Equipment is understandable and sparse.

Outside, establish one distinctive native plant species repeated at different depths. Tall dark pewter-grey stems are fluted rather than covered in familiar tree bark, with irregular swollen joints. At a few joints, two opposing fans of thick curved ribbon-shaped leaves extend almost horizontally. Upper surfaces are muted blue-green, undersides smoky plum. Each leaf has a consistent structural rib and taper, with natural age and weather damage. Near the water, stems divide into three or four substantial buttress roots. Show several complete silhouettes clearly; background individuals repeat the same botanical form at believable smaller scale. Avoid palm crowns, bamboo canes, Earth conifers, giant mushrooms, luminous plants or a collage of unrelated alien species.

The hut stands on engineered piers above the seasonal flood level. One raised service walkway starts visibly beside it and bends between roots toward exactly two small research habitats in the middle distance. A continuous nonslip deck, supported spans and usable railings lead into actual entrance vestibules. Compact sealed habitats have rounded rectangular ends, recessed warm windows and simple rain canopies. Protected utility conduits follow brackets beneath the walkway. Foundations avoid major roots. Leave gaps in the vegetation so the connected settlement can be understood; buildings must not look pasted into an impenetrable jungle.

Rain-dark shallow water beneath the structures has subdued reflections and root islands. Fine rain traces remain outside; separate soft mist layers recede between nearer stems and the far woodland. Keep open atmospheric regions for later animation, not an opaque fog wall or giant plume. The room stays dry, with faint window reflections and no oversized foreground droplets. Geography and weather agree across every pane. No celestial body is visible through this overcast forest: native ecology and flood-adapted colony architecture establish the off-world identity.

Soft cool rainy daylight supplies most illumination. The desk lamp and occupied habitat windows create small inviting pools of warmth. Finish the clean artwork with rich readable shadows, gentle highlight roll-off, distinct matte surfaces, clear middle-distance shapes and softer distant detail. Avoid flat grey lighting, orange/teal grading, neon foliage, glossy plastic, HDR halos or extreme sharpening. Wear belongs at touched edges and exposed wet joints; broad surfaces remain quiet and maintained.

The mood is a sheltered shift maintaining contact between field teams and their homes on an unfamiliar living world. Human-scale doors, useful furniture and credible engineering anchor the alien landscape. No people, creatures, spacecraft, holograms, decorative machinery, tangled wiring, duplicated props, warped furniture, impossible supports, huge planets, lettering, branding, watermark, UI or border. Output one complete clean cinematic photograph, not a collage or poster.
