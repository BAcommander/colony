## Lore anchors

Lexicanum describes the Blood Angels' Baal stronghold as a fortress-monastery occupying an extinct volcanic formation with two peaks. The caldera carries its enclosing Arx Murus wall. An armoured transparent roof, the Dome of Angels, protects the interior; the slender Archangelian rises from the perimeter. The article cites The Devastation of Baal and First Founding for these architectural details. It also distinguishes the fortress's earlier history, wartime devastation and later expansion. We have not consulted those complete books, so these details remain attributed to the encyclopedia rather than independently checked against their text. [Source](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Arx_Angelicum)

An identified surface/moon depiction would require additional reference rather than treating this generic disc as a verified astronomical portrait.
