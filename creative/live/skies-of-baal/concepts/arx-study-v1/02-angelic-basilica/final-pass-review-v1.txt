RECOMMENDATION
Use Option A first: a restrained material-and-construction pass. The composition has enough spectacle already. The strongest visible defect is the swirled/embossed texture spread across the foreground stone; further additions would not fix it. Keep the skyline, enlarged world, single eagle, quiet terrace and one ruined precinct.

OBSERVED PRIORITIES
1. Foreground stone: unnatural labyrinth-like relief covers many unrelated block faces and floor slabs. Replace it with broad matte faces, subtle mineral grain, believable joints and wear concentrated at edges. Preserve lamp spill and the shapes of the blocks.
2. Main cliffs: larger fractures now read better than the earlier melted/knotted texture, but some fine noise and masonry/rock transitions remain ambiguous. Resolve load paths, bridge footings and distinct material edges. Do not make everything equally sharp.
3. Ruin: the gateway adds a useful middle-distance focal point. Improve uneven sand burial, foundation contact and the partly erased route; keep it unlit and modest.
4. Depth: separate near grit, middle sand and distant atmospheric forms through detail and contrast, retaining open space. The ruin must remain subordinate to the living fortress and sky.
5. Sky: retain v4's clearer disc and small cloud overlap. All local clouds lie in front of the distant body. No transparent cloud passing through the solid disc and no halo cutout around it.
6. Celestial illumination: v4 does not establish a physically verified phase/orbit. Preserve the selected cinematic depiction for A; a deliberately astronomy-led revision would need an explicit inferred sun direction and chosen phase. Do not claim the disc is a verified portrait of a named moon.
7. Foreground habitation already works: bench, blanket and warm lantern communicate a place to rest. Extra machinery or a visible Marine would shift the contemplative mood.
