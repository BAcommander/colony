Somewhere beyond the shipping lanes, one terrace still keeps its light.

The Crimson Watch overlooks a desert settlement built into ancient cliffs. Across the plain, a weathered gateway marks a road the dunes have almost reclaimed. Warm rooms face the gathering clouds. Above them, a distant world hangs in the last clear light.

An imagined place for quiet work, reading and a little distance from the day.
