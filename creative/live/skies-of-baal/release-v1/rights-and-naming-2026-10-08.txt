SKIES OF BAAL — NAMING AND RELEASE RIGHTS
Checked 8 October 2026. General information and a practical project recommendation, not legal advice or a clearance opinion.

RECOMMENDATION
Keep Skies of Baal as the working name of the existing tribute. It fits the emotional idea. For a commercial Ambient Colony release, prefer a materially original setting, provisionally The Crimson Watch, or obtain specific permission covering the actual AI-assisted artwork, animation and intended monetisation. The user has not selected either release route. Do not silently rename or alter the existing art.

OFFICIAL POLICY CHECK
Games Workshop's current Intellectual Property Guidelines distinguish non-commercial human-created fan artwork from licensed fan animation. The policy excludes generative-AI fan content from its permitted categories, bars monetisation of setting-based fan content, and reserves fan films/animations to licensing. Its fan-art conditions also restrict copied official imagery and logos. Read the actual policy for its full terms:
https://www.warhammer.com/en-GB/legal#IntellectualPropertyGuidlines

The official page was read directly in the browser, including its Generative artificial intelligence, Fan-artwork, Underlying rights and Infringements sections. The text-fetching search tool returned 403; the live official page was accessible in the in-app browser. No policy revision date was displayed in the inspected section. This is an access date, not a claim about when the terms changed.

APPLICATION TO THIS PROJECT
The saved art deliberately represents Baal and the Arx Angelicum, was made with image generation, and used franchise visual references. A new musical composition and custom animation do not independently clear those visual/setting issues. Do not describe this package as officially licensed, canon-accurate, copyright-free or compliant with GW's fan-content policy.

A disclaimer identifies the project honestly but does not grant a licence. Turning off ads would not resolve the AI and animation policy objections. Avoid treating donations, memberships or sponsor placement as a workaround. An original setting would need changes to recognizable symbols, distinctive design and lore as well as the title; see original-colony-option/requirements.txt.

Names and branding also raise trademark/endorsement questions separate from copyright. This review did not search trademark registers or establish ownership of the words Skies of Baal by themselves. It does not decide whether a particular generated visual legally infringes. The policy is the rights holder's position, not a court ruling.

UK legal exceptions are limited and context-specific. A relaxing entertainment livestream is not automatically criticism, review, parody or fair dealing because it is a tribute. For a firm decision about this exact artwork and commercial plan, consult an IP solicitor or seek written permission before public release:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright
https://www.gov.uk/using-somebody-elses-intellectual-property/copyright
