# Skies of Baal — concept brief v1

## Emotional promise

Give the listener time beneath the sky Ignis wished to see. The vast view carries longing; a small pool of inhabited warmth gives it human scale. Mournful, tender and quietly beautiful, with enough harmonic life for extended listening.

An imagined peaceful homeworld view inspired by the line. Original art and instrumental music supply the experience. The quotation remains a creative reference; no episode dialogue, footage or existing score samples in the proposed repeating asset.

## Visual direction: the last watch gallery

The fixed camera sits at seated eye level in a modest enclosed observation gallery. A broad sealed pressure-rated window looks onto a rust-red eroded basin, pale mineral shelves and thin distant dust. The huge twilight sky takes roughly the upper two-thirds: copper at the horizon, dusty rose cloud edges, muted violet and cold indigo above. Recognizable broad clouds have quiet gaps and real depth. Lighting stays subdued and coherent.

Heavy stone piers, a recessed window and a maintained metal sill give the refuge a restrained fortified character. A supported protected service route reaches a low inhabited complex with a few warm windows. Enclosure and access respond to the abrasive world. One connected refuge gives the view purpose.

In the lower corner: a worn empty chair, plain handleless beaker and warm practical lamp. Someone has stepped away. Keep the room uncluttered; the sky is the subject. No battlefield, dying body, heroic armour figure or moving focal character proposed.

Atmosphere, eroded terrain and adapted shelter establish the off-world identity. A small moon must not carry it alone. Weather, celestial placement and architecture are artistic choices, not a verified canonical skyline. Do not label this invented gallery as a particular Baal landmark or present its history as established lore.

Follow [the colony art direction](../../concepts/ALIEN_COLONY_ART_DIRECTION.md): environmental signature, consistent world cue, adapted construction and habitation. Distinguish this from Saltline through the sky-dominant gallery composition, pale eroded terrain and memorial warmth.

## Music direction

Warm elegiac space ambient, beatless and spacious. Textured analog pads carry the middle register; a soft bowed-string-like synth supplies restrained ache; isolated rounded piano notes suggest memory. Diffuse reverb gives distance while the close voice remains intimate. Intended timbres, not descriptions of a recording.

Two sustained voices change notes independently. Minor-leaning and suspended harmonies open occasionally into gentle consonance. A sparse two- or three-note thought returns with changed spacing, register and response, leaving long rests. Modest centred bass, audible middle voices at low volume and smooth treble.

Move from longing toward shelter, then into unresolved openness. Avoid recurring grief climaxes, triumphant resolution, a funeral march or an endless single-note rumble. A listener arriving anywhere should feel held by the music.
