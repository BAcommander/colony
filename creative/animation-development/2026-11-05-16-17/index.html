Cyberpunk Apartment

Refine the existing city systems, then add a few longer stories.

01 Cloud banks travelling between the towersCore

Move coherent cloud shapes across several skyline depths, with clear gaps and near towers staying sharp.

Method: Source-texture transport and separately renewed gap mist; preserve both source taps and destination silhouettes. V3 has translating density patterns, not a complete source-cloud transport pass.

Pass when: A recognizable cloud edge moves at full-scene720p without dragging a tower or producing a flat fog stripe.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

02 Convincing irregular road trafficCore

Clear vehicle bodies and distinct head/tail lights follow the existing near and elevated roads, with pairs, gaps and safe headways.

Method: Replace normalized10s whole-route travel with source-space speed and explicit event births/occlusion. Perspective-scaled silhouettes, premultiplied edges, contact/reflection response. Preserve the removed hovering-car repairs.

Pass when: Traffic reads as vehicles on asphalt at normal size; no overtaking collisions, curb-following errors, floating lights or repeating short tile.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

03 A rare commuter shuttle on the elevated roadSupport

One longer illuminated bus crosses the existing elevated roadway, briefly disappearing behind towers.

Method: Road vehicle, not a new railway or aircraft. Low-contrast body with a few attached windows and wet-road response; one passage in a proposed3-5min interval.

Pass when: The body remains aligned with the road and correctly occluded, and the larger transit moment feels occasional.

Preparation: New small vehicle asset or a carefully projected procedural body; inspect its geometry before a full pass.

04 Independent sign programmes with local light spillCore

A few existing large signs have readable travelling heads, dimmer holds and different rhythms.

Method: Map complete existing emitters including white cores; preserve glyphs. Give each sign its own schedule and drive nearby haze and surfaces from that same signal.

Pass when: At least two signs visibly change in the full scene; city exposure and building edges remain fixed.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

05 Reflections that belong to each light sourceCore

Selected sign and traffic changes echo in the river, wet roads and the cool floor strip.

Method: One emission signal per source with separate perspective-mapped receiver masks; calm source-detail-preserving water motion. Retain warm furniture reflections.

Pass when: Colour, timing and apparent source position agree; river motion has no shoreline tearing or broad floor wobble.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

06 Refractive droplets with trails and occasional mergingCore

Several readable close glass drops bead, stick and slide while finer rain stays outside.

Method: Replace a few flat bead marks with local source-valid refraction, a narrow highlight and a fading wet trail; selected merge events need continuous volume/paths.

Pass when: Drops survive normal-size viewing; refraction never samples a frame or interior object and trails do not cover the view.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

07 Ventilation vapour in the lower citySupport

Two anchored exhaust plumes rise from verified rooftop outlets, then drift into the existing street mist.

Method: Choose actual flues in the source before drawing anchors; broad overlapping wisps, wind-linked drift and building occlusion.

Pass when: Plumes clearly leave their outlets and stay behind intervening masonry; no emission from an invented antenna.

Preparation: Trace actual visible outlets first; if no convincing outlet is present, retain this as gap mist instead.

08 Better cup steam against the dark sofaSupport

Soft uneven wisps leave the existing cup, separate and disperse rather than drawing a repeating corkscrew.

Method: Keep the cup and table fixed; broaden the useful vapour footprint and vary density independently of the neon programmes.

Pass when: Visible but quiet at720p, attached to the liquid surface, with no repeated spiral or smoke-like plume.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

09 Occupied windows and existing mast lightsSupport

A few complete apartment windows hold different occupied states; selected existing mast lights have readable small bright centres.

Method: Trace larger real panes and hardware instead of scattering random dots. Most windows stay steady; source-mounted beacon halos remain bounded.

Pass when: Window states are local and independently held; no whole-district breathing or lights placed in haze.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

10 One warm practical with coupled reflectionsSupport

The table lamp has an occasional gentle hesitation, shared by its nearby pool and marble reflection.

Method: Reuse the Crater lamp core/spill method with new source geometry. Include the white bulb; keep most room lighting steady and use a long attention clock.

Pass when: Core, nearby table and warm reflected contribution dip together; no room-wide exposure change or constant flicker.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

11 A few leaves moving in indoor ventilationExperiment

Only selected leaf tips bend gently from their attachments while stems, pot and sofa remain anchored.

Method: Use isolated leaf elements over a clean background; do not warp the whole plant or smear the scene behind it. A constrained test earns inclusion.

Pass when: No doubled original leaf, rubbery edge or displaced pot; the movement is subtle and credible at source scale.

Preparation: Requires leaf separation and a clean revealed background patch; existing V3 deliberately keeps the plant static.

12 An occasional river service ferryExperiment

A small utility boat crosses a verified open-water route with a narrow wake and a soft reflected light.

Method: Map a true water corridor, bridge/building occlusion and perspective before making an asset. Keep the passage sparse over several minutes.

Pass when: A solid hull sits on water with a restrained wake; no crossing dry terraces, emerging through a bridge or bright floating dot.

Preparation: New vessel asset plus independently reviewed water/occlusion masks; optional after the main city pass is accepted.

SCENE 17 · PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT

The Gate Camp After Sunset

Make the open gate light a living expedition camp.

Numbers mark candidate locations. Core = first visible build; support = second pass; experiment = separate asset/geometry proof.

01 A steady watery portal with broad rolling motionCore

The open surface rolls slowly with darker pockets and pearl-blue highlights, retaining fine texture underneath.

Method: Convert V2 into a real60s periodic surface with bounded source-valid deformation and matched boundary velocity. Avoid a spinning texture or repeated activation burst.

Pass when: Broad motion reads in the full scene without glitter noise, ring deformation or a detectable reset.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

02 Portal light that reaches the surrounding setCore

The same surface wave lights stair treads, rail edges and nearby stone; a restrained blue rim reaches appropriate camp objects.

Method: Use the portal emission field for hand-mapped receiver masks with material/facing/falloff differences. The warm lantern remains the foreground counterlight.

Pass when: Blue movement is visible on real surfaces at720p, follows the portal and does not wash the whole frame blue.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

03 Cloud layers crossing the alien skyCore

Cloud edges travel behind spires and in front of selected distant haze while the large world and small moon stay fixed.

Method: Separate source-safe sky runs and planet/spire exclusions; advect coherent texture and hide local renewals.

Pass when: Recognizable travel is visible across the open sky; neither world nor rock fragments enter moving samples.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

04 Wind in depth: basin air and low ground streamersCore

Distant basin haze moves slowly while an occasional nearer dry-air tongue crosses the approach around the rocks.

Method: Depth-specific sizes/speeds and shared gust cues; stone, steps, path lamps and the camp chair occlude the ground layer.

Pass when: Far and near movement are both readable, with no straight fog boundary or opaque band across the valley.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

05 Canopy fabric breathing between its supportsCore

A soft gust lifts the broad cloth folds while seams, poles and fixed attachments hold their positions.

Method: Start with a source-bound mesh deformation of interior cloth folds. Free-edge movement requires a clean revealed sky patch and a separate test.

Pass when: A broad fold visibly responds to wind without dragging poles, stretching seams or revealing a duplicate cloth edge.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

06 A loose strap and selected scrub responding to gustsExperiment

A verified loose strap twitches and one small scrub clump leans with stronger gusts.

Method: Distinguish free webbing from taut guy lines. Separate movable tips over clean background where silhouettes move; keep the motion subordinate to the canopy.

Pass when: The same gust drives cloth and vegetation; tensioned lines remain believable and no original silhouette is doubled.

Preparation: Requires precise movable-element isolation; omit a component that cannot be cleanly reconstructed.

07 Warm lantern and path-light presenceSupport

Keep a strong warm place to sit, with a rare short practical-light dip and attached spill on canvas, case and ground.

Method: Trace complete white cores and local lit surfaces. Most path lights stay steady; any lamp event uses a long independent clock.

Pass when: Warm core and spill respond together without fire-like flicker on an electric lantern or synchronized path flashing.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

08 Blue surface reflections on the ring and rail hardwareSupport

Portal ripples add moving cool highlights to the inner ring lip, rails and appropriate carved metal.

Method: Tight material/facing masks driven by G01. Preserve the already-open gate state and steady warm chevron identity.

Pass when: Metal reads as lit metal with stationary carvings; no floating halo, moving glyphs or mechanical redial every minute.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

09 Live survey map on the laptopSupport

A small route marker, restrained scan and occasional status-line refresh animate the existing map.

Method: Perspective-map inside the current screen; retain its original map texture and bezel. Use expedition telemetry rather than a prominent decorative waveform.

Pass when: Readable activity stays inside the angled display; no laptop, keyboard or floor movement.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

10 A radio receive indicator on the real deviceSupport

A source-mounted receive indicator and small meter acknowledge occasional communications.

Method: Select a real screen/lens on the device; use short held acknowledgements on a long event clock, optionally linked to a later radio SFX audition.

Pass when: Light is attached to hardware and survives720p; no random key sparkle or mandatory chatter.

Preparation: Existing clean plate; new source masks must be traced at native size.

11 Mug steam after a small geometry repairSupport

A few fine warm wisps rise from a correctly formed mug and disperse into the open air.

Method: Repair the duplicated handle first, freeze the new plate/hash, then trace cup/rim and use irregular source-anchored steam.

Pass when: One coherent mug handle, correct rim attachment, visible quiet steam and no changes outside the repair/effect masks.

Preparation: The existing source has handles on both sides; prepare a confined repair before this effect or any final plate lock.

12 A rare expedition rover near the approachExperiment

A small wheeled survey rover makes a quiet approach along verified clear ground and settles by the expedition staging area.

Method: Requires a convincing asset, projected wheel contact/suspension, local shadows and solid rock/rail/chair occlusion. Keep it a rare narrative beat.

Preparation: New reviewed rover asset and source-space ground/occlusion setup. Do after the base environment is accepted; defer human portal crossings.
