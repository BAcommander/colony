## Idea and visual direction

Eye-level from a modest deck-chief maintenance office behind reinforced internal glazing looking onto an immense enclosed military carrier hangar. Strong 2004 Battlestar Galactica atmosphere: heavy gunmetal bulkheads with clipped corners, dense overhead steel ribs, worn yellow deck lines, practical suspended fluorescent work lights, an original small stub-winged colonial-style aerospace fighter parked at rest with service panels open. Two secured tools and a physical checklist clipboard beside an old desk telephone and simple coffee cup in the office, ordinary padded chair. Warm small office lamp versus cold cavernous flight deck, no glossy spacecraft hotel. Pressure-secured internal blast doors in far wall; NO open hangar to vacuum, no combat and no characters. Photographic documentary material response, convincing bay scale, fighter landing supports visible, tools sparse and meaningful. Original fighter and hangar composition, no insignia, readable markings or logos.

Thumbnail headline: **BETWEEN SORTIES**. This is an experimental short mood headline, not a confirmed public title. No catalog number assigned.
