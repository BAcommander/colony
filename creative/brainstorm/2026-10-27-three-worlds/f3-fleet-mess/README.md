## Idea and visual direction

Small civilian transport mess-room within a tired surviving fleet, 2004 Battlestar Galactica atmosphere of military-civilian endurance and human-scale scarcity. Ordinary worn bolted canteen table, two simple seats, small thermos, patched pale-grey and muted burgundy wall panels, steel ribs and small warm service lamp. A modest reinforced observation window frames one immense battered armoured carrier with long side flight-pod-like structures and three widely spaced smaller civilian freighters drifting against black space; original fleet silhouettes, carrier not an exact Galactica replica. Sparse cold running lights, dominant near carrier and small human refuge, no battle or explosions, no nebula or giant planet. Pressure enclosure coherent, all floor objects obey common local gravity. Original composition, no logos, emblems or text. Restrained photo exposure: warm tired interior against cold immense fleet, not polished futuristic lounge.

Thumbnail headline: **STILL TOGETHER**. This is an experimental short mood headline, not a confirmed public title. No catalog number assigned.
