## Idea and visual direction

From a quiet sheltered observation balcony inside a vast ancient ocean city, looking through huge closed elegant angled windows. Recognisable Stargate Atlantis atmosphere: slender tiered bronze-silver towers, turquoise/blue inset light channels, fanlike geometric architectural tracery and copper-toned ribbed columns, graceful vertical city spread across a supported star-like marine platform. City sits low above immense cold dark alien ocean, storm cloud horizon and soft blue evening light. Foreground is a small warm occupied-looking science watch corner: one chair, one mundane modern expedition laptop and unbranded supply case contrasting with elegant ancient surfaces. Near piers and connected decks explain supports, sealed interior dry and habitable. Light from city towers is sparse not neon skyline. Original tower silhouettes and viewpoint rather than an exact TV set. One dominant luminous tower group against ocean, restrained practical warmth at human scale. No people, text or logos, no ringed planet.

Thumbnail headline: **A CITY FAR FROM HOME**. This is an experimental short mood headline, not a confirmed public title. No catalog number assigned.
