## Idea and visual direction

An empty late-1990s urban safehouse on an upper floor in the Matrix simulation's visual atmosphere. A single black corded landline telephone on a plain scratched table beside one worn chair is the foreground focal point. Flaking pale olive paint, restrained geometric tiled wall divisions, old vertical venetian blinds and a broad rain-streaked closed window overlooking repeated office-window grids and a dim empty street far below. Broad green-olive colour bias in light and material, desaturated sickly fluorescent glow, deep controlled blacks, mundane architecture made subtly uncanny by repetition. One small warm bulb kept very restrained, no cosy orange flood. Rainy nocturnal city and phone dominate; original room, no copied movie lobby or characters. No falling code over scene, no red/blue pills, guns, leather coats, holograms, giant neon billboards or readable signs. Photographic thriller still with realistic phone shape and continuous table/chair geometry.
