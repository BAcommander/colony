## Idea and visual direction

View from beneath a small expedition field-station canopy at blue hour on an alien plateau. One ordinary rugged laptop with subdued screen, folded canvas chair, a handleless enamel beaker, secure radio and canvas equipment case occupy one quiet foreground edge. Across a short clearly walkable path an immense upright ancient circular transit gate stands on a supported weathered stone plinth, recognisable Stargate SG-1 atmosphere: thick dark carved metal/stone ring with angular chevron-like locks, a calm luminous blue water-like surface held vertically inside. No emerging splash. Pale otherworldly eroded spires and a broad distant moonlit mineral basin beyond, expedition lantern and warm camp window versus gate blue. Sympathetic modern field hardware alongside immense ancient technology, grounded photographic late-evening exposure, believable gate base and maintained access, powerful simple ring silhouette. No people, military insignia, logos or franchise text; original camp/site arrangement. Not a fantasy magic circle, no glowing runes everywhere.
