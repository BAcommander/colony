## Idea and visual direction

Inside a centuries-old deep-space transit chamber with strong Stargate Universe/Ancient design atmosphere. Weathered dark bronze structural ribs and warm age-darkened metal form a grand but human-accessible rectangular chamber; angular carved panel divisions and sparse amber inset light guide a level walking route to one thick ancient circular transit ring with restrained cool blue inner surface. Off-axis composition from a small modern expedition watch desk with a simple physical radio, chair and one subdued laptop. Contrast improvised human occupation against ancient functioning machinery. A closed panoramic side observation slit reveals cold starfield and faint curved planetary horizon, no Earth landmarks. Ring is the dominant silhouette; visibly anchored to substantial floor supports, connected chamber walls, clear pressure enclosure and safe route, maintained practical materials. Original arrangement, no exact Destiny set, franchise logos or text, no decorative fantasy arch or all-over neon.
