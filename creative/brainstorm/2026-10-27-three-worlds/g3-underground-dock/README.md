## Idea and visual direction

From a small patched human maintenance refuge looking through reinforced closed inspection glass into a vast underground hovercraft dry dock. Strong Matrix real-world/Zion atmosphere: immense cavernous dark rock shaft, circular heavy industrial service galleries with thick visible supports and warm amber practical pinlights, enormous blue-grey patched hovercraft suspended in secured maintenance cradles above an enclosed dry repair platform. Foreground one warm task lamp, an ordinary seat, physical gauges and no clutter, cold damp stone exterior to room while foreground floor stays dry. Sparse blue-white ship service lamps and amber human gallery lights, no green wash in this real-world space, no futuristic clean white spaceship or hologram. The single dark hovercraft silhouette against immense layered shaft is readable. Original ship and dock composition, no literal copied Zion shot, no robots/characters, readable text or emblems. Believable supports/access and photographic textures.

Thumbnail headline: **BENEATH THE MACHINE**. This is an experimental short mood headline, not a confirmed public title. No catalog number assigned.
