How to interpret this

LUFS differences can be corrected with gain and are not evidence of better composition. Frequency balance and stereo are distinct from harmony, phrasing and perceived comfort. Tonal variation is not a proven loop. Use the level-matched listening experiments below before choosing a new production recipe.
