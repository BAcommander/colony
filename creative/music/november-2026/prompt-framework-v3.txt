Purpose: continuous scene ambience for long, voluntary listening. Original direction informed by first-pass user preference, practitioner research and current ElevenLabs documentation; generated results still need testing.

1. State instrumental mode and actual requested length.
2. Give a sound family, free-time/beatless direction and one sentence of scene mood.
3. Assign a rounded foundation, a near texture and a distant layer. Keep their identities consistent.
4. Describe slow changes of colour, voicing, density and depth. Keep musical events sparse and soft, rather than writing a sequence of little questions and replies.
5. Keep overall continuity while allowing gentle unequal internal changes. Avoid a rigid volume cycle or named dramatic structure.
6. Keep bass controlled, midrange softly audible and top end smooth. Describe a usable sonic relationship, not a numerical spectral quota or compulsory fixed note.
7. Keep environmental sound outside the Music pass so it can be mixed and tested separately.
8. End open and still active; no required cadence or fade to silence. Inspect the export because prompting cannot guarantee that ending or a seamless loop.
9. Use one compact exclusion sentence for actual failure modes. Avoid endlessly adding prohibitions that remove all life.
10. A long prompt preserves the chosen owned reference and atmosphere. Change one layer gradually, without scheduling a new event or section every minute.

What this does not establish: reference instruments/key/BPM, Ambient Outpost's exact process, a universal ambient genre definition, a guaranteed model response, a proven focus/sleep effect or all-day acceptance.
