## Production prompt

Create a ten-second cinematic ambient loop from the original Ringfall observatory still. Keep the locked camera, architecture, chair, rug, desk, one-handled mug, landscape, colony geometry and planetary rings intact. The room should feel occupied moments ago: quiet, warm and operational. The image remains the master plate; all movement comes from independent controlled layers.

Foreground: a continuous translucent column of coffee steam rises directly from the mug opening. Give it a narrow coherent origin, widening curls, layered fine wisps, changing gaps and soft breakup. Vapor always rises and fades naturally. It must be readable against the wall at ordinary viewing size, without looking like thick smoke, a glowing rope or repeated identical blobs. Preserve the mug rim and lamp.

Laptop: preserve the existing lunar image and interface. Add an understated moving scan sector over its lunar display and small telemetry motion, locked to the screen perspective. The screen stays dim and readable, never a bright flashing panel. Nothing changes outside the display.

Colony: make the distant base feel active using two independently timed warm pools of work-light moving across the existing ground, respecting the terrain texture and rock occlusion. Include restrained sequential activity in a few existing windows and a slow mast beacon. Use surface illumination only, with no visible shafts or dust in the lunar vacuum. Keep the light movement spatially readable, not simply a global brightness pulse.

Planet: retain its exact silhouette, terminator, rings and original broad stripes. Layer a separate low-contrast flowing cloud texture within the protected atmospheric mask. Continuous directional phase flow and gentle band shear should suggest massive slow atmospheric circulation. No generated replacement planet, expanding disc, reversing whole texture, new storm spot or contour drift. This is the slowest supporting movement, not the sole source of visible life.

Different layers have different rhythms; nothing pulses in unison. Keep highlights controlled, shadows stable and the original warm/cool balance. No spacecraft, added furniture, people, arbitrary star twinkle, camera movement, full-frame dissolve or exposure pumping.

First render and inspect a full-scene 720p preview and detail samples. Validate protected pixels and per-layer periodic endpoints separately from visual quality. Build the loop from periodic layer functions, keeping steam transport upward across the boundary. Export 300 frames at 30fps, exactly ten seconds, silent. If the preview shows no obvious visual defect, make a 3840x2160 review export from the same layers and timing, clearly identified as upscaled source artwork and pending user artistic approval.
